About

Where strategy
meets science

I am a PhD-trained technologist and delivery leader who has spent over 15 years at the intersection of machine learning, data systems, and people-first management. Today I lead complex data platform programmes at Munich Re, one of the world's largest reinsurers — driving decisions for hundreds of underwriters across three continents.

My superpower is translating deep technical complexity into clear outcomes — for engineers, for executives, and for the people in between. I've built teams from scratch, secured programme funding, negotiated enterprise contracts, and created cultures where diverse talent thrives.

I am a proud Latina in STEM, an advocate for diversity and inclusion in tech leadership, and a firm believer that the best ideas come from the most unexpected people — when you build the right environment for them.
