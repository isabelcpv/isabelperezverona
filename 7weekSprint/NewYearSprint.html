7 Week Sprint

I’m opening a small case study sprint for three people who have a business idea they can’t stop thinking about, but haven’t been able to validate properly yet.

In seven weeks, we’ll take the idea, cut the fluff, test the riskiest assumption with real people, and make a clear go / no-go decision before you waste months building something or quit your job too early.

But this is not one of those “new year, new me” things where you wait until January, write a big resolution, and then spend three more months thinking about the same idea.

You do this now so you start the new year already walking.

By the time everyone else is still deciding what they “might build this year,” you’ll already have your idea shaped, your test planned, your pitch sharpened, and the first real-world signal in motion.

This round is small on purpose. I’m not doing a big group where you watch videos, fill templates, and disappear into your own confusion. You’ll have private pitch work, talking to your real customers, and a final debrief with me, so I can actually look at your idea with you and help you read the signal properly.

The promise is simple: by the end of the year, you’ll know if your business idea is worth chasing, without quitting your job.

That means no more collecting ideas forever. No more going to networking events, founder talks, growth events, and coming home with more energy but no clearer answer.

You’ll take the idea out of your head, shape it into something small enough to test, put it in front of real people, and get a proper pulse read.

Is this just an idea, or is there a business here?

That’s the question we’re answering.


I’ve spent the last 10 years inside corporate environments taking fuzzy ideas and turning them into something people can actually test. I also have a PhD in computer science and machine learning.

I don’t say that to sound fancy. Fancy is boring.

I say it because this is my jam: take the messy thing, break the complexity, find how to test it, and make the decision clearer.

This is not for everyone.

This is not for you if you’re still asking, “Do I even want to be an entrepreneur?” I’m not here to convince you to start. And it’s not for you if you want to hide forever, avoid talking to people, or protect your idea keeping it in the bottom of a drawer.

Also, if you’re terrified that people know you asked three people a smart question about a business idea, this will probably feel too spicy.

But if you already have the fire, if you know you can do more, if you’re working regularly and spending your evenings going to events, taking notes, playing with ideas, joining communities, reading the books, listening to the podcasts, trying to become “that person”... this is built for you.

Especially if the part you’re stuck on is not motivation.


Here is how we will do this:

First, we clear the overload. I’ll take you through my Stack Slasher method, where we get the pile of ideas out of your head and reduce the noise fast. By the end of that first block, you’re not trying to validate 17 versions of the business. You’ll have a strong direction in front of you, so we can stop pretending that “more ideas” is the answer.

Then we shape it. We take the fluffy version and make it smart, measurable, actionable and small enough to test in 7 weeks. This is where most aspiring founders get it wrong. They try to test the whole dream. We’re going to test the piece that gives you signal.

After that, we build your stage. That means finding the right people, the right market pocket, the right environment to show it. You don’t need to go full power mode. You need the right small test in front of the right people.

Then we create your curiosity test and founder pitch. You’ll have templates and AI prompts, so you’re not staring at a blank page wondering how to say the thing.

We’ll tune the message together before you send it, so it’s clear, useful, and safe enough to put out there without blowing up your reputation.

At the end, we collect the responses and do the pulse read. We’ll crush the answers together, look at the signal, and decide what the research is actually telling us. Pursue, pivot, or park.

No drama. Just data.

As soon as we kick off, you will get The Pulse Map, so you can see the full path and know where you are each week.

You’ll get weekly missions, so you’re not waking up each week wondering what to tackle next. Every mission moves the idea one step closer to real-world signal.

You’ll get the templates and AI prompts, so the work moves faster and cleaner without getting stuck.

Every week we will have one weekly call where we can check progress, remove the mess, and set the next target.

For this case study round, I’m also adding three extra support pieces so you’re not doing the scary parts alone.

First, you’ll get a Pitch Safety Review before anything goes out. I’ll look at your founder pitch with you and help make it clear, specific, and safe enough to send — so you’re not accidentally announcing a half-baked startup to your whole network.

Second, you’ll get a Stage Builder Map, where we work out who should actually see the test first. Not “post it everywhere and hope.” The right small pocket of people, so the feedback means something.

And at the end, you’ll get a Go / No-Go Decision Debrief with me. We’ll separate real signal from polite “sounds interesting” replies, and you decide whether this is pursue, pivot, or park. And I will include a custom AI prompt for mapping the next step.
