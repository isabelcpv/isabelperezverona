Why Raising Bicultural Kids is a Systems Design Problem

Most people treat belonging as something given to them by a place, an institution, or a culture. But then, why does fitting in seem to come naturally to some, while for others it feels like an uphill battle?

Is there an explanation why the place you live — and plan to raise your family — just doesn’t feel right? That is the whole point behind this space: to understand what belonging really means across different dimensions, and implement a system that allows you to fit in without losing your identity.

BELONGING

What means Belonging

That feeling of being an outsider may have a data-informed explanation — and that is why I believe belonging can be engineered. This matters especially for families raising children in a country different from the one they grew up in. My mission is to uncover the patterns behind belonging and turn them into practical frameworks for designing lives and communities where people can thrive without losing their identity.
